Volunteer With Us

Formation is delivered by people. Every role below supports structured programs — with one screening standard across all of them.

The Standard

One Screening Standard. Every Role.

Every volunteer in any role — program-facing or not — completes background check and LiveScan fingerprint screening, reference checks, and a code of conduct. Volunteers in contact with youth or families additionally complete mandated reporter training. Formation roles receive structured training before serving a cohort.
