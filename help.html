Formation Pathways
How Can We Help?
01

I'm a First Responder or Agency Leader

Sovereign Shield™ (law enforcement) and Sovereign Station™ (fire service) formation for your department. Dual-badge architecture built for Canyon Lake's founding public safety agencies.

02

I'm a Parent or Youth

The Grit to Genius™ founding cohort at Canyon Lake — ages 12–17, three age bands, twelve seats per band. Fall 2026 pre-registration open.

03

I'm a Family Member, Survivor, or Veteran

Formation for the households that carry the weight — grief, blended family, caregiver, and veteran capacity building pathways beginning in Canyon Lake.
