How It Works

Discovery to Continuity.

01 · Discovery

A conversation about your population, your constraints, and whether the fit is real. No pitch deck.

02 · Design

Which program, which cohort structure, what the implementation asks of your organization — stated flatly before anything begins.

03 · Deliver & Measure

Structured delivery with entry-to-exit measurement where the program supports it. Aggregate reporting — never an individual's results.

What LID Brings

Licensed curricula, trained and screened facilitation, measurement instruments, privacy architecture, and the charitable funding lane that removes cost as the barrier.

What Partners Bring

The population, the venue, the institutional trust, and a named point of contact who can move a decision.
