This organization is in its founding year. This page reports commitments and progress — never a plan dressed up as a result.

What We Measure

Entry to Exit, in Aggregate.

Where a program supports it, participants complete structured measurement at entry and at exit. What gets reported publicly is aggregate — seats delivered, completion rates, and capacity change across a cohort. An individual's results are never published, and nothing a participant writes inside a program reaches their agency, school, or family.
