"In darkness" is not a description of the people we serve. It is a description of where the work happens. The three a.m. call. The smoke. The night shift. The house no one else will enter. We go in. Those Who Respond

First Responders

Those Who Are Rising

Youth · Ages 12–17

The founding youth cohort at Canyon Lake — three age bands, twelve participants per band. Identity, capacity, and courage built early through the Grit to Genius™ formation program.

Families · Survivors · Veterans

Formation for the households that carry the weight — families, survivors, and veterans building the capacity to sustain themselves and each other under pressure.
