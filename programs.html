The Founding City Project

Who We Build

Three formation pathways — capacity, readiness, and deposits made before the withdrawal comes. All curricula and instruments licensed from EQGenix Inc.

Door One · First Responders
Those Who Respond

Law enforcement and fire service under one dual-badge architecture — formation for two founding Canyon Lake public safety agencies, from line personnel to command.

Sovereign Shield™

Twelve-module law enforcement formation curriculum. Submitted for California POST certification review. Self-paced delivery through structured LMS, covering cognitive sovereignty, emotional regulation, resilience architecture, and relational intelligence.

Sovereign Station™

Twelve-module fire service formation curriculum built for the unique operational tempo, culture, and identity pressures of firefighters and fire leadership. Structured formation from academy through command.

Door Two · Youth · Ages 12–17
Those Who Are Rising

The founding youth cohort at Canyon Lake, beginning Fall 2026. Identity, capacity, and courage built early — before the pressure arrives.

Grit to Genius™

A six-week youth formation program building cognitive sovereignty, emotional regulation, and effort tolerance through structured, age-appropriate modules. Three age bands (12–13, 14–15, 16–17), twelve participants per band.

Structure & Safety

All facilitators undergo background check and LiveScan screening, reference checks, and mandated reporter training. Parental consent and youth assent required. Life in Darkness does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical services.

Founding Cohort

Canyon Lake, Fall 2026. Limited to 36 seats across three age bands. Pre-registration open for Canyon Lake families through the contact page.

Door Three · Families · Survivors · Veterans
Those Who Hold the Line

Formation for the households that carry the weight — families, survivors, and veterans building the capacity to sustain themselves and each other under pressure.

Family Formation

Structured capacity building for families navigating blended households, grief, caregiver strain, and relational fracture. Sacred Deposits™ workshop programming available in the charitable lane.

Grief & Loss

Structured formation pathways following loss — building the emotional architecture to carry what remains and build what comes next.

Veteran & Survivor Formation

Capacity building for veterans transitioning to civilian identity, survivors navigating post-crisis reconstruction, and the families who hold the line alongside them.
