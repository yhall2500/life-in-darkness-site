Our Story

Born from Darkness.
Built for Light.

Life in Darkness builds emotional and cognitive capacity upstream of crisis — formation for the people who respond, the people who are rising, and the households that hold the line.

Leadership

Yvonne G. Hall

Founder & Executive Director, Life in Darkness

Life in Darkness was founded from a single conviction: that emotional and cognitive capacity should never be a luxury reserved for those who can afford it, especially for the people who respond to crisis, the young people rising into it, and the households that hold the line through it.

Its founder, Yvonne G. Hall, is a curriculum developer, applied behavioral researcher, and NAMA CAMS-II Certified Anger Management Specialist with training in technology, social and political science, and psychology. She has led initiatives across both the private and nonprofit sectors, including work with at-risk populations and service as a former Guardian ad Litem in Florida and California courts.

Yvonne is also the Founder and CEO of EQGenix Inc., the emotional and cognitive wealth advisory firm whose frameworks and instruments Life in Darkness licenses. She is a federal prime applicant with applications pending before the Department of Justice (OVW, COPS) and has submitted Sovereign Shield™ for California POST certification review. Across both organizations, her leadership brings private-sector rigor, structure, and accountability into community-facing work.

Married, a mother of seven, grandmother of twelve, and a woman of faith, Yvonne's life work centers on walking alongside people in their hardest moments — not to fix what is broken, but to help them build what comes next.

Two organizations. Two missions. One belief: human capacity is built, not borrowed, and no one should be excluded from the building.

What We Believe

Our Convictions

Darkness is a Doorway

The hardest seasons of life are not endpoints. With the right support, they become the foundation of your greatest strength. We meet people in the dark and walk with them toward what's next.

Capacity Over Coping

We don't teach people to merely survive. We build the emotional and cognitive architecture that allows them to thrive, well beyond the moment of crisis. Builders, not borrowers.

Access is a Justice Issue

Structured capacity building should not be gated by income, insurance, or geography.

Community Is Capacity

Isolation amplifies every crisis. Connection, authentic, non-judgmental, and sustained, is one of the strongest protective factors we know of. Everything we build is designed to foster it.

The Framework

Science Meets Compassion

Every Life in Darkness program integrates neuroscience research, emotional regulation tools, and character development practices into structured, accessible pathways. Our core frameworks, licensed from EQGenix Inc., have been tested across corporate, education, and community settings before reaching the people we serve.

This isn't self-help. It's capacity-building, giving people the cognitive and emotional architecture to not just survive their crisis, but to emerge from it fundamentally stronger.

How It Works

From Crisis to Capacity

Whether you come to us in crisis or seeking growth, our process meets you exactly where you are and moves at your pace.

Reach Out

Connect through the contact form or by phone. We route you to the formation pathway that fits.

Get Matched

We assess your needs and connect you with the right door, whether that's grief support, family formation, or youth programming.

Begin Your Pathway

Enter a structured program designed around structured frameworks. Each pathway has clear milestones, and practical tools you keep..

Build Forward

Transition from recovery to resilience. Our goal is never dependence, it's capacity. You leave with skills, community, and a foundation that holds.

Life in Darkness builds capacity upstream of crisis. It does not provide clinical diagnosis, treatment, therapy, or crisis response. People in acute crisis are routed to 988 and to licensed providers.
