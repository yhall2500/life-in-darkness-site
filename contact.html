Crisis Resources
If You Need Help Right Now

Life in Darkness builds capacity upstream of crisis. If you or someone you know is in acute crisis, please contact these verified resources immediately.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988
Available 24/7.

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741
24/7 text-based crisis support.

SAMHSA National Helpline

Call 1-800-662-4357
Free referrals and information, 24/7.

Riverside County Crisis Line

Call 951-686-7450
Local crisis support for Riverside County.
