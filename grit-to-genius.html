The Barrier We Remove

Course Facts

The Specification,
Stated Flatly.

Structure

A six-week formation cycle — structured weekly sessions with a beginning, a middle, and an end. Not a drop-in program.

Age Bands

Three bands: 12–13, 14–15, 16–17. Age-calibrated curriculum in each.

Cohort Size

Twelve participants per band — thirty-six seats total in the founding cohort.

Measurement

Entry assessment and exit measurement across the five capacities. Results belong to the family, never published individually.

Safeguarding

Background check + LiveScan for every adult, mandated reporter training, written parental consent and youth assent.

Founding Cohort

Canyon Lake, Fall 2026. Pre-registration open to Canyon Lake families.

Developed by EQGenix Inc. Delivered to Canyon Lake families at no cost by Life in Darkness.

The Program
01

What Participants Build

Grit to Genius™ builds cognitive sovereignty, emotional regulation, and effort tolerance — the capacity to think independently, stay composed under pressure, and stay with hard things. Three age bands (12–13, 14–15, 16–17), twelve participants per band.

02

Structure & Safety

All facilitators undergo background check and LiveScan screening, reference checks, and mandated reporter training. Parental consent and youth assent required. Life in Darkness does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical services.

03

Founding Cohort

Canyon Lake, Fall 2026. Thirty-six seats across three age bands. Pre-registration open to Canyon Lake families.

Safety and Screening

How We Protect Your Child

Background Screening

All facilitators and volunteers undergo background check and LiveScan fingerprint screening, reference checks, and are subject to a code of conduct.

Mandated Reporting

All adults in contact with youth participants complete mandated reporter training and are bound by California reporting obligations.

Parental Consent

Written parental consent and youth assent are required before participation. Parents receive program details, session schedules, and facilitator information in advance.

Scope of Service

Life in Darkness does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical services. Acute concerns are routed to 988 and to licensed providers. This is formation, not intervention.

Life in Darkness builds capacity upstream of crisis. It does not provide clinical diagnosis, treatment, or crisis response. People in acute crisis are routed to 988 and to licensed providers.
